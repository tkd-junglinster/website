Twice a year the club holds a belt grading, where students demonstrate the
techniques, forms and spirit they have worked on during the term and test for
their next belt.

Gradings are a proud moment for students and families alike, and everyone is
welcome to come and support. Your coach will let you know if you are ready to
grade and help you prepare.

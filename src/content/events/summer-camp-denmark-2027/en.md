Every summer, taekwondoists from across Europe come together in Denmark for a
week of intensive training, new friendships and a lot of fun. It is one of the
biggest events on our calendar and a fantastic experience for children, teenagers
and adults alike.

## What to expect

- Training sessions with experienced international instructors
- Activities for all ages and levels — beginners are very welcome
- Evening activities, games and social time
- A chance to train alongside clubs from many different countries

## Who can take part

Members of all ages and grades. Younger children travel and stay with their
families or with club supervision — we will share full details closer to the time.

The Lënster Maart is Junglinster's big autumn market and festival at the Centre
Culturel Am Duerf, with artisans, regional producers and a lively programme over
two days.

## Good to know

- **Friday 19 September:** 16:00–23:00
- **Saturday 20 September:** 10:00–20:00
- Food and drink stalls run by local associations
- Children's activities with AnimaKIDS (magic shows, mini-disco, face painting,
  bouncy castles)
- Live performances and DJs across both days
- Free shuttle every 15 minutes from three car parks during opening hours

A great weekend out for the whole family — come and say hello.

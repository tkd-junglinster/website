Lënster wibbelt is a community sports and leisure afternoon at the Centre
Sportif Op Fréinen, where local clubs run taster workshops and activities for
all ages.

## What's on

- Try-out sessions in taekwondo and many other sports (gymnastics, badminton,
  basketball, table tennis, climbing, football, tennis and more)
- A "Food Village" with local, regional and fair-trade refreshments
- A fun park with inflatables and games
- Collect stamps from four workshops to enter a raffle for a €50 sports voucher
  (drawn at 20:30)

Our club runs a taekwondo workshop — a perfect chance to bring a friend and
show them what we do. Public transport stops right outside at *Jonglënster –
Op de Fréinen*.

The traditional Adventsmaart returns to the square in front of the Centre
Culturel. Come along and see what the local associations have to offer as the
festive season begins.

## Good to know

- **Saturday 29 November 2025**, 11:00–23:00
- Stalls and refreshments from local clubs and associations
- The LGS St. Martin will again offer traditional advent wreaths
- Organised by Adventsmaart-Lënster a.s.b.l.

A warm, festive evening out — a lovely way to start Advent together.
